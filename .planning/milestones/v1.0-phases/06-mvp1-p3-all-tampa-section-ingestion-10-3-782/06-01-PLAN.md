<objective>
Establish the target-generation layer and prove the full all-Tampa ingestion path end-to-end on ONE
bounded subject before scaling to all ~212 subjects.

This is the tracer for Phase 6. It (1) creates a reproducible generator that rebuilds the full
~1,402-entry `config/course_targets.toml` from the git-ignored `courses.csv` snapshot and commits
that config (locked user decision 1: git-tracked config is the source of truth, reconciling the
5-entry config against the 10 stored courses into the full list), then (2) drives the EXISTING,
unmodified per-subject ingestion CLI for the single subject CHM — chosen because CHM contains the
canonical CHM 2045 / CHM 2045L suffix pair — through catalog ingest, schedule ingest, the merged
campus + suffix guards, a data-quality gate, and the honest-coverage contract, all against the live
hosted Supabase DB.

Purpose: prove the architecture on the best early-context tokens. If the generated config, the
per-subject batch unit, the guards, or the honest-coverage contract are wrong, we find out after one
subject (~46 bounded requests), not after 212.

Output: `scripts/generate_tampa_targets.py`, a committed full `config/course_targets.toml`, a Wave-0
generator test, a generalized suffix-guard regression test, and CHM ingested end-to-end with 0
quality errors.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Singular -> plural transition (a handful of target courses -> ALL Tampa). Is the course/target
identity model still correct at full scale? **Decision: no-change.** Rationale: identity is already
`(subject, number)` on `CourseTarget` and term/CRN/source on stored rows; scaling to ~1,402 courses
is purely additive under the same keys, and suffix variants are already distinct `(subject, number)`
rows in `courses.csv` (CHM 2045 vs CHM 2045L). No identity promotion or add-alongside is needed.
</assumption_delta_decision>
</objective>
