<identity_model>
## Identity model — resolved before the plural expansion

This phase moves from a two-course, 46-section sample to every subject offered at Tampa for the
target term. That singular-to-plural move needs one identity question answered first, or the
resulting counts are meaningless.

**Question:** when the scope becomes every subject, what identifies a section, and what stops a
section from being counted twice?

**Answer, taken from the existing schema rather than invented:** a section's identity is
`(term, CRN)` — `src/easy_a/models/sections.py` declares `UniqueConstraint("term_id", "crn")`.
A course's identity is `(subject, number, catalog_edition)`. A grade row's identity is
`(term_id, crn, source)`.

**Consequences this plan implements:**
- Per-subject counts record both rows seen and distinct CRNs, so cross-listed sections surfacing
  under two subject queries are visible instead of silently doubling the total.
- Report-level section totals count distinct CRNs across complete subjects, never summed rows.
- The subject is the batching boundary, not the identity: a subject with zero Tampa rows for the
  term is a real observation (`complete` with zero sections), not a missing record.
</identity_model>
