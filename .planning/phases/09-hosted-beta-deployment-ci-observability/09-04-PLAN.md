<objective>
Build the fetch half of a sweep: one whole-term request, memory-bounded parsing that fails closed, the D-04 undergraduate Tampa scope with the D-03 breakdown, the pure sanity gate, and the transaction-scoped advisory lock.

Purpose: REQ-SYNC-01 needs one whole-term Tampa request per sweep and a sanity gate that aborts a short, empty or reshaped response before any write. Research showed the real response always ends in a USF error tail. It also showed the naive parse peaks at 443 MB, against a 512 MB worker, while chunked parsing peaks at 121 MB. Session advisory locks do not survive the transaction pooler.
Output: `search_term`, `easy_a.sync.fetch`, `scope`, `gate`, `lock`, a synthetic fixture builder, tests.

Reversibility (flagged, costly, D-04): the undergraduate-only scope rule (first four digits below 5000) touches the sync filter, coverage reporting and D-21 exception accounting if widened or narrowed later. It lives in one function, is_undergraduate_number, reused by the validators (09-10), to keep that cost contained.

Recorded for user review (research Open Question 2): USF lists 359 undergraduate cancelled sections (secondary status U or C) plus 10 with an unlabelled status R. They are present in the sweep, so they are not "removed" and stay searchable as today. This plan does not change that and adds no label for R. Hiding or labelling cancelled sections is a separate one-line decision for the user.
</objective>
