The Easy-A score summarizes past grades and withdrawals. With less history, it moves toward a broader average to avoid overstating a small sample. A higher score means more favorable past outcomes.

Courses are sorted by this calculated score. The percentage on each course is the share of recorded A–F grades that were As; it is not the score or your chance of earning an A. See the score inside “See grades & sections.” Courses without their own letter-grade history appear last in this design.

Historical outcomes describe past classes. They do not predict your grade.
